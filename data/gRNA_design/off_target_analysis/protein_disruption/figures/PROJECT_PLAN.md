# Arabidopsis CRISPR Knockout Project Plan

## Project Title
In-silico CRISPR/Cas9 guide RNA design and functional knockout analysis of NPR1 and PAD4 genes in Arabidopsis thaliana.

---

## Objective
To design high-efficiency CRISPR guide RNAs targeting early exons of NPR1 and PAD4 genes and predict functional consequences of gene knockout.

---

## Phase 1 – Gene Retrieval
- Retrieve genomic and CDS sequences from TAIR
- Identify exon-intron structure
- Select early exon targets

---

## Phase 2 – gRNA Design
- Use CRISPOR for guide design
- Select top 3 guides per gene
- Evaluate GC content and on-target score

---

## Phase 3 – Off-target Analysis
- Genome-wide off-target scanning
- Select guides with minimal off-target sites

---

## Phase 4 – Functional Disruption Analysis
- Simulate frameshift mutation
- Translate mutated CDS
- Predict premature stop codon

---

## Phase 5 – Documentation and Reproducibility
- Maintain structured repository
- Upload all intermediate results
- Ensure reproducible workflow
